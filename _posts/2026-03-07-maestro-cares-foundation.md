Maestro Cares Foundation is a non profit organization founded in 2012 by renowned singer Marc Anthony and entrepreneur Henry Cárdenas. Since its inception, the foundation has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to improving the quality of life for children and communities in vulnerable situations across Latin America and the United States. With over 42 projects implemented in 17 countries and territories, the organization has invested more than $13 million in infrastructure, education, and essential resources that have positively impacted the lives of more than 15,000 children and adolescents.

![Image](/images/posts/maestro-cares-foundation/31c9f477-fdba-80aa-9838-ffbbc41c4d61.jpeg)

Marc Anthony, co-founder and emblematic figure of the foundation, has used his influence and artistic platform to draw attention to the most pressing social causes. His commitment goes beyond simple philanthropy; Anthony actively participates in fundraising events, including the Annual Golf Tournament and the Annual "Changing Lives & Building Dreams" Gala held in New York. These initiatives not only generate financial resources but also raise awareness about the importance of investing in the future of the most underserved communities.

![Image](/images/posts/maestro-cares-foundation/31c9f477-fdba-8056-b808-def586eaeef0.jpeg)

The foundation has succeeded in bringing together numerous celebrities and influential personalities who share the vision of creating a lasting impact on society. These high-profile collaborators not only contribute financial resources but also lend their voice and presence to amplify the message of hope and transformation that Maestro Cares promotes. Through exclusive events such as the golf tournament in Miami, these public figures inspire others to join the cause and demonstrate that social change is possible when efforts are united.

![Image](/images/posts/maestro-cares-foundation/31c9f477-fdba-80d7-8dcc-f97e5c0221ef.jpeg)

The programs of Maestro Cares Foundation encompass multiple critical areas for the comprehensive development of children. Among its most notable initiatives is the university scholarship program, which breaks down the financial barriers that prevent talented and socially committed students from accessing higher education in the United States, Puerto Rico, and Latin America. Additionally, the foundation invests in educational infrastructure, building schools, foster homes, and community centers that provide safe and healthy spaces where children can live, learn, and play.

![Image](/images/posts/maestro-cares-foundation/31c9f477-fdba-80bb-913b-cdede060d0d6.jpeg)

Collaboration with local organizations and governments is fundamental to Maestro Cares' working model. This strategy ensures that projects not only respond to immediate needs but also generate sustainable long term impact. The foundation focuses on strengthening entire communities, providing not only physical infrastructure but also educational programs, healthcare, and resources that empower families to become agents of change in their own environments.

![Image](/images/posts/maestro-cares-foundation/31c9f477-fdba-8090-bf47-e0ef7cee5eb3.png)

The legacy of Maestro Cares Foundation continues to expand thanks to the support of donors, volunteers, and collaborators who believe in its mission. Each event, each project, and each transformed life represents another step toward the vision of creating a world where all children, regardless of their origin or circumstances, have access to the opportunities they deserve to reach their maximum potential and become tomorrow's leaders.
