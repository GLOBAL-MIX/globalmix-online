# Business Class

In the competitive world of premium aviation, there exists an airline that has redefined the concept of accessible luxury. Boliviana de Aviación doesn't just connect continents; it transforms every flight into a sensory experience where each detail has been meticulously designed for the contemporary traveler who values both comfort and sophistication. The Miami to Santa Cruz route in Business Class represents the perfect fusion of aeronautical innovation and South American hospitality.



![Image](/images/posts/boa-business/3119f477-fdba-800a-b41a-e4547fcedce4.webp)



The Business Class cabin of the Airbus A330-200 is an intimate space of just 20 seats strategically distributed in a dual aisle configuration. Each seat is a masterpiece of ergonomic engineering: seats that transform into fully flat 180° beds, equipped with state of the art touchscreen panels that respond to the slightest touch. The extendable footrest and intuitive controls allow you to personalize your space until you reach that exact point between relaxation and productivity during transatlantic journey.



The onboard digital experience rivals the finest home cinema systems. Each seat features a personal high definition touchscreen offering an extensive entertainment catalog: from the latest film releases to exclusive series, music from all genres, and interactive maps that transform the flight into an immersive experience. Strategically located charging ports ensure your devices remain powered at all times, allowing you to stay connected or work without interruptions.



![Image](/images/posts/boa-business/3119f477-fdba-8017-a51e-cdba2cbaabde.webp)



Boliviana de Aviación's culinary offering elevates the concept of airline food to gourmet dimensions. The menu, curated by specialized chefs, presents a selection that celebrates both international cuisine and authentic South American flavors. Each dish is accompanied by a carefully selected premium wine list, artisanal cocktails, and a variety of beverages that perfectly complement every moment of the flight. The meal service isn't simply sustenance; it's a gastronomic experience at 35,000 feet.



What truly distinguishes Boliviana de Aviación is its ability to offer genuine luxury without the traditional prohibitive Business Class price tag. As the most affordable option in its category within South America, the airline democratizes premium comfort. The generous baggage policy, 3 checked bags, 1 carry on, and a personal item, along with priority boarding and luxury amenities, create an unbeatable value proposition. The personalized service from the crew, always attentive yet discreet, ensures every need is anticipated before it arises.



![Image](/images/posts/boa-business/3119f477-fdba-8018-9317-e52c583af46d.jpeg)



Choosing Boliviana de Aviación's Business Class is a statement of principles: it's possible to travel with style, comfort, and sophistication without compromising your budget. For the contemporary traveler who understands that true luxury lies in the details, in the quality of rest, in service excellence, in the freedom to carry everything necessary, this is the definitive option.
