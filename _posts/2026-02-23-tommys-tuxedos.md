Since 1976, Tommy's Tuxedos has been synonymous with masculine elegance in Miami. With over 45 years of dressing gentlemen for the most special moments of their lives, this iconic house has become a pillar of formal fashion, offering distinction, personalized service, and meticulous attention to detail that makes all the difference. Tommy, the founder and heart of the brand, remains the expert guide who helps each client find the perfect suit thanks to his deep knowledge of cuts, fabrics, and proportions.



![Image](/images/posts/tommys-tuxedos/3109f477-fdba-80e6-bc6f-f5787b42d767.png)



Leading the team is Luis Casella, key figure at Tommy's Tuxedos, who brings a modern and dynamic business vision that has allowed the brand to maintain its excellence and close connection with clients. His leadership has established Tommy's Tuxedos as a trusted name not only in Miami, but far beyond its borders. By his side, his wife Elizabeth Cataldo is the creative mind and a true reference of refined taste on the most prestigious red carpets. Elizabeth is the one behind the looks that dazzle at events such as the Martín Fierro Latino Awards, Premios Lo Nuestro, and the sophisticated Miami & New York Fashion Week.

Elizabeth's influence transcends borders: she has participated in red carpet coverage at the 2023 Academy Awards and at Fashion Week in London and Paris, in addition to dressing celebrities such as Adamari López and Susana Giménez at various high profile events in Miami. Her ability to highlight each person's individuality while maintaining sophistication is what elevates every experience and distinguishes Tommy's Tuxedos from other rental houses.



![Image](/images/posts/tommys-tuxedos/3109f477-fdba-80f8-9669-fe220d8110d9.jpeg)



The experience at Tommy's Tuxedos goes far beyond simply renting a suit. With an extensive catalog of designs and accessories, personalized service, and professional tailoring, the boutique offers comprehensive solutions for every need: from sizes up to XXL and options for children and youth, to same day service and cleaning included with the rental. While the team can efficiently respond to last minute requests, it is recommended to make reservations in advance to guarantee the best selection and availability.

What truly distinguishes Tommy's Tuxedos is having professionals who understand the art of dressing well. Elizabeth is not just a fashion expert, she is a professional with an intuitive sense of elegance, style, and proportion. As a trusted partner at high profile events in Miami, South America, and Europe, Tommy's Tuxedos brings an extra touch of distinction that ensures every appearance reflects confidence and timeless style.

![Image](/images/posts/tommys-tuxedos/3109f477-fdba-803a-b6c6-e72c14951b31.png)



With multiple convenient locations in Miami and Miami Lakes, Tommy's Tuxedos continues to be the first choice for those seeking quality, tradition, and impeccable style. Every suit, every detail, and every interaction reflects the commitment of more than four decades of making their clients' special moments unforgettable.
