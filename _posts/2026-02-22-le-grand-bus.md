London has always been synonymous with elegance, history, and unforgettable experiences. Now, there's a new way to discover the city's iconic landmarks that combines luxury, comfort, and sophistication like never before. Le Grand London Tea Bus is redefining sightseeing in the British capital, offering visitors a truly unique journey through the heart of one of the world's most magnificent cities. This isn't just a tour, it's an experience that blends the grandeur of London with the finest hospitality on wheels.



![Image](/images/posts/le-grand-bus/3109f477-fdba-807b-ac75-c98932b503b1.jpeg)



What sets Le Grand apart is its stunning double decker bus, the only one of its kind in the world. Featuring a 360° panoramic glass roof, passengers can enjoy breathtaking views of London's skyline from every angle. The interior is nothing short of spectacular, with elegant designer sofas and armchairs, an onboard kitchen, air conditioning, a restroom, Bose sound system, and Wi-Fi. Every detail has been carefully curated to ensure that guests travel in absolute comfort and style. With a capacity for 32 passengers, the bus maintains an intimate atmosphere that makes every tour feel exclusive and personal.



![Image](/images/posts/le-grand-bus/3109f477-fdba-804b-865d-f9d132cfde71.jpeg)



The service on Le Grand is as exceptional as the vehicle itself. Guests can choose from two bespoke itineraries: the Best of London Tour, which showcases iconic landmarks such as Big Ben, Westminster Abbey, and Hyde Park, or the Tower (City) of London Tour, featuring the Tower of London, Tower Bridge, and London Bridge. Each tour lasts approximately 1 hour and 40 minutes and includes a smart audio guide available in six languages; English, French, Russian, Chinese, German, and Spanish. This ensures that every guest can fully immerse themselves in the rich history and fascinating stories behind London's most celebrated sites.



![Image](/images/posts/le-grand-bus/3109f477-fdba-80c3-857c-e4d56d7427cf.jpeg)



But Le Grand isn't just about sightseeing, it's about indulging in a complete sensory experience. The bus offers an exquisite à la carte menu featuring gourmet bites, refined desserts, fine wines, champagne, and cocktails. For those seeking a more traditional British experience, the French Afternoon Tea is an absolute must. Inspired by French culinary traditions, it includes a selection of delicate canapés, desserts, and hot drinks paired with champagne. As they say at Le Grand, "It's never too early for champagne and never too late for afternoon tea." This culinary journey transforms the tour into a truly unforgettable gastronomic adventure.



![Image](/images/posts/le-grand-bus/3109f477-fdba-80b7-ae9e-dd08ac99b62c.jpeg)



Le Grand also caters to private events, making it the perfect choice for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, VIP transfers, and more. The team works closely with clients to create tailored experiences that suit any occasion. Whether it's an intimate celebration or a grand corporate gathering, Le Grand ensures that every detail is perfect. The bus can be hired for private tours anywhere within London's M25, offering endless possibilities for customization. From the route to the menu, everything can be personalized to create a one of a kind experience that will leave guests talking for years to come.



![Image](/images/posts/le-grand-bus/3109f477-fdba-800f-a113-e10d446ae426.jpeg)



With departures from Piccadilly, Green Park, Constitution Hill, Bus Stop E, Le Grand London Tea Bus has quickly become one of the most sought after experiences in the city. Starting from just £49 per person, it offers incredible value for an experience that combines luxury, culture, and world-class service. Whether you're a first time visitor to London or a local looking to rediscover your city in style, Le Grand promises an unforgettable journey through the capital's most iconic streets.
